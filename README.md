# 📉 Attrition Analysis
  ## Overall Attrition
- **Total Attrition:** 237 employees
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%

This indicates that nearly 1 out of every 6 employees leaves the organization.

## Attrition by Department
  ### Department	Attrition Contribution
  - Research & Development	65.37%
  - Sales	30.34%
  - Human Resources	4.29%
  ### Insights
  - Research & Development has the highest employee turnover.
  - Sales also contributes significantly to attrition.
  - HR department maintains comparatively stable retention.
  ### Business Impact
  **High attrition can lead to:**
  - Increased hiring costs
  - Productivity loss
  - Knowledge drain
  - Reduced employee morale
